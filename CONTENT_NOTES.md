# Content and evidence notes

This repository presents selected optical-bench, instrument-integration and modeling work by Jun Young Hong.

- Photographs and experimental figures are author-supplied research material. Their inclusion does not imply prior journal publication.
- Actual measurements, calculated results and planned changes are labeled near the figures. No synthetic replacement is used as experimental evidence.
- PCB resonator geometry is based on Sasaki et al. as attributed in the detailed page. Literature topology is not claimed as original invention.
- Raw experimental captures, full manufacturing Gerbers, complete chip layouts, native commercial-solver files, lab configuration and old repository history are omitted.
- Implementation source is retained privately. This public portfolio presents methods, selected results and workflows; it is not a complete reproducibility archive.
- No new open-source license is assigned. The selected material is shared as a technical portfolio; detailed original research archives are not included.
